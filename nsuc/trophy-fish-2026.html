Trophy Claim Rules

  1. Fish must be caught in accordance with all rules, regulations and laws.
  2. The weighing of the fish must be on certified or good quality digital scales, photographed, and witnessed — witness name listed on the claim.
  3. A video (preferred) or photograph of the weight reading with the fish on the scales must be supplied.
  4. To be eligible for a trophy or record, the claimant must be a financial NSUC member.
  5. A claim must be made to and recognised by the Sports Secretary within 1 week of capturing the fish (or arriving back on land).
  6. The fish must be shot without assistance as per record rules — no assistance from a boat, another diver, or a second shot.
  7. Social/Comp trophies must be shot from a launch site within the Alliman zone (Bass Point to Norah Head).
  8. All trophy claims must include a photo of the diver holding the fish.
